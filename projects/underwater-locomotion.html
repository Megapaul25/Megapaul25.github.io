Underwater Robot Locomotion

Bio-inspired robotics · Fluid simulation · Locomotion
● Ongoing project

Overview

This ongoing project explores robotic locomotion in aquatic environments, with a focus on the interaction between robot dynamics and surrounding fluid.

My current work focuses on developing and investigating simulation approaches that can be used to study the behavior of robots moving through water.

The project is still in progress, and the simulation setup and its results are being actively investigated.

Current Work

Fluid Simulation

Exploring fluid simulation methods and their integration with robotic simulation to study fluid–body interactions.

Simulation Integration

Working on coupling fluid dynamics with MuJoCo-based robotic simulations, with the goal of testing different configurations.

Locomotion

Investigating how robot motion and body dynamics interact with the surrounding aquatic environment.

Validation and Debugging

Testing the simulation pipeline, investigating numerical issues, and examining the computed forces and torques.

Technical Environment

The work involves extending and testing simulation workflows to better represent the interaction between robotic bodies and fluid environments.

Python MuJoCo LilyTorch Fluid Dynamics Numerical Simulation Robotics Debugging

Next Steps

The next steps involve improving the reliability of the fluid–robot coupling, investigating the resulting forces and torques, and establishing a more robust simulation setup for studying underwater locomotion.

As the project progresses, this page will be updated with validated results and further technical details.
