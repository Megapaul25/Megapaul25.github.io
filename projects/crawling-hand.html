Robotic Crawling Hand

Robotics research · Simulation · Hardware integration

This project is subject to a non-disclosure agreement. The description is intentionally limited to general technical aspects.

Overview

As part of a robotics research project, I contribute to the development and testing of a robotic crawling hand. The work combines simulation, hardware integration, and experimental prototyping.

My work involves connecting software and physical hardware, testing robotic behaviors, and improving the reliability of the overall system.

Further details about the design and application are omitted due to confidentiality requirements.

My Contributions

Simulation

Working with MuJoCo to develop and test robotic behaviors in simulation, supporting development before and alongside physical experiments.

Hardware Integration

Integrating software with motors, sensors, and embedded controllers to operate the robotic system.

Prototyping

Contributing to the practical development of the robotic platform through hardware assembly, CAD-related work, and prototyping.

Testing and Debugging

Testing system behavior, investigating hardware and software issues, and refining the integration between system components.

Technical Environment

The project involves both simulated and physical robotic systems, requiring coordination between control software, embedded electronics, and mechanical components.

MuJoCo Python Robotics Simulation Motor Control Sensors Embedded Systems CAD Prototyping

Objectives and Learning

This project gives me hands-on experience with research-oriented robotics development, particularly the challenges of bringing together simulation, mechanical design, electronics, and software.

It also allows me to work across the boundary between virtual testing and physical experimentation, where integration and debugging are essential parts of the development process.

Confidentiality

In accordance with the project's NDA, detailed technical specifications, internal implementation details, and non-public results are not disclosed.
