About me

I am pursuing a Master's degree in Robotics at EPFL, after completing a Bachelor's degree in Microengineering. My main interests are mobile robotics, legged locomotion, and bio-inspired robotic systems.

I enjoy working across simulation, control, software, and hardware, from implementing locomotion algorithms to integrating sensors and actuators on physical robots.

I am particularly interested in robots designed to explore challenging environments, including underwater, mountainous, and other environments where conventional robotic systems struggle.
